
## Limits of Binary Representation
**Determine the limits of binary representation:**
   - Largest positive number with 8 bits in unsigned binary  -Ans: 255(Binary:11111111) 
   - Largest positive number with 8 bits in signed binary (two's complement)  -Ans: 127(Binary:01111111) 
   - Smallest negative number with 8 bits in signed binary (two's complement)  -Ans: 128(Binary:10000000)
