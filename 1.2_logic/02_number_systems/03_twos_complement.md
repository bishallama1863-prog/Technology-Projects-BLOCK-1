## Two's Complement
**Find the two's complement of the binary number:**
   - 101010
     Ans:
     Invert (1's Complement): 010101
     Add 1 (2's Complement): 010110
   - 110011
     Ans:
     Invert (1's Complement): 001100
     Add 1 (2's Complement): 001101
   - 1001
     Ans:
     Invert (1's Complement): 0110
     Add 1 (2's Complement): 0111
